talk_title: Advancing Instruction through Computer Vision
talk_abstract: In this talk, the application of computer vision and AI to video analysis of the elementary classroom will be detailed. The talk will explain data collection, video scoring, illustration of results on a custom dashboard as well as a novel video coach for teachers. The use of multimodal inputs including video, audio, and text will be explored. Studies showing the efficacy of the computer vision methodology will be summarized. The talk will conclude with a demonstration of the AI for Advancing Instruction tool.

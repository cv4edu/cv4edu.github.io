talk_title: Computer Vision for Classroom Observation - Privacy, Bias, and Multimodality
talk_abstract: Computer vision and AI have significant potential to enhance teaching quality by providing teachers with detailed, frequent, objective feedback on their teaching. Despite enormous progress in machine perception, automatic classroom observation remains a challenging sociotechnical problem.  In this talk I will present three projects related to this research theme: (1) computing semantics-preserving anonymous representations of classroom videos to promote sharing but preserve privacy; (2) the potential for "mathematical correctness bias" in training ML and CV systems based on educational datasets; and (3) the nuanced benefit of multimodality in transcribing speech in learning scenarios.

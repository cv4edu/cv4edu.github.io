**Abstract**: Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) offer significant potential for video understanding, yet their application in education remains primarily focused on student assistance rather than teacher support. This paper presents a sequential MLLM-based framework that analyzes the audiovisual content of YouTube videos against the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS). This system assists educators in identifying classroom-appropriate materials by evaluating nuanced criteria, including vocabulary level, topical relevance, the presence of advertisements, and specific NGSS alignment. To validate the framework, 247 science videos were evaluated against expert human assessments. The system achieved 92.7% agreement with human experts, demonstrating its effectiveness in identifying concrete science concepts. This architecture provides a scalable solution for any educational domain governed by established standards.

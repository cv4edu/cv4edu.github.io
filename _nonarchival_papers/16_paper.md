**Abstract**: We present EduLens, a system pairing teacher-worn smart glasses with a self-evolving AI agent for real-time adaptive teaching support. Our contributions are threefold. First, we propose an end-to-end architecture where on-device CV models estimate student engagement via gaze, pose, and affect analysis, feeding a knowledge-grounded AI agent that delivers context-aware pedagogical suggestions through the heads-up display, with an action detection module that flags misalignments between suggestions and actual outcomes. Second, we introduce a scenario taxonomy of five classroom situation categories the agent must handle: engagement recovery, student-specific targeting, content & pacing adaptation, interaction design, and teacher behavior coaching. Third, we propose a self-evolution protocol with a human-in-the-loop teacher review (approve/revise/reject) and a dual test-suite training session, where regression suites guard prior fixes and resolution suites validate new ones, ensuring the agent improves monotonically across evolution cycles.

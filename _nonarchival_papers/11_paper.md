**Abstract**: AutoOEP is a locally-deployable multi-cue framework using a dual-camera setup to monitor both the student and their workspace. It integrates facial analysis, prohibited object detection, and hand-object interaction features, which are processed by an LSTM to capture temporal behaviour and estimate cheating probability. Evaluated on a custom dataset, AutoOEP achieves \textbf{90.7%} accuracy and an F1-score of \textbf{92.7%}, while reducing false positives by \textbf{6.4 times} compared to a single-frame baseline. The system operates at 2.37 fps on a CPU-only setup, supporting practical deployment in resource-constrained settings.
